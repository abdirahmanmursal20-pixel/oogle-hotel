OOGLE HOTEL Policies

These standards are designed to protect guests, staff, the hotel property, the mosque, the conference hall and the dining area. Written booking terms provided by OOGLE HOTEL take priority if they differ.

Respectful, Safe and Comfortable Hospitality

OOGLE HOTEL welcomes families, business travellers, organisations and visitors to Ceel-Afweyn. Guests are expected to respect other guests, staff, local customs and hotel property. Management may refuse or end a stay when safety, serious misconduct, unlawful activity or major disruption is involved, subject to applicable law.

Full Hotel PoliciesPrivacy PolicyWebsite Terms

Reservations & Confirmation

  • A booking is confirmed only after OOGLE HOTEL confirms it by phone, email or written message.
  • Guests should provide accurate names, room type, dates and number of occupants.
  • Group and conference bookings may have separate written terms.

Check-in, Check-out & Identification

  • Check-in and check-out arrangements are confirmed with the reservation.
  • Early arrival or late departure is subject to availability.
  • Management may request appropriate identification for adult guests where reasonably required for safety or records.

Cancellation & No-show

  • Cancellation terms are provided when the booking is confirmed.
  • Please notify the hotel as early as possible if travel plans change.
  • No-show or group/event terms may differ where stated in the booking confirmation.

Payments & Pricing

  • Room, food and hall prices are confirmed directly by OOGLE HOTEL.
  • This public website does not collect card or bank details.
  • Only use payment instructions supplied through official OOGLE HOTEL contact channels.

Room Occupancy, Children & Visitors

  • Room occupancy must stay within the number agreed at booking.
  • Children must be supervised by a responsible adult.
  • Visitors must respect guest privacy, staff instructions and hotel security requirements.

House Rules & Quiet Enjoyment

  • Noise should be kept at a respectful level, especially during rest and prayer times.
  • Harassment, threats, fighting, serious disturbance or abusive behaviour are not accepted.
  • Guests should keep rooms and common areas reasonably clean and use facilities responsibly.

Smoking, Alcohol & Prohibited Items

  • Smoking is not permitted inside guest rooms, the mosque, dining area or conference hall.
  • Alcohol and unlawful substances are not permitted on hotel premises.
  • Hazardous materials and unlawful items are prohibited.

Mosque Etiquette

  • The mosque is a respectful prayer space and must be kept clean and quiet.
  • Guests should follow appropriate prayer-area etiquette and staff guidance.
  • Food, disruptive activity and misuse of the prayer space are not permitted.

Somali Dining & Allergies

  • Menu items and availability may change according to supply and season.
  • Guests should tell staff about allergies or dietary needs before ordering.
  • The kitchen may handle common allergens and cannot guarantee a completely allergen-free environment.

Conference Hall & Events

  • The hall must be booked in advance and used within the agreed purpose and capacity.
  • Organisers are responsible for their guests, setup and respectful use of the space.
  • Extra cleaning, damage or special setup may be charged if agreed in the event booking.

Damage, Keys & Lost Property

  • Guests may be responsible for deliberate, reckless or significant damage they cause, subject to applicable law.
  • Lost keys or hotel property should be reported promptly.
  • Found items may be kept for a reasonable period; collection or delivery arrangements are the guest's responsibility.

Safety, Emergencies & Special Requests

  • Guests must follow reasonable safety instructions and must not interfere with safety equipment.
  • Report hazards, maintenance problems or emergencies to hotel staff immediately.
  • Accessibility or special assistance requests should be discussed before arrival so the hotel can confirm what is available.

Privacy & Guest Information

  • This website currently sends booking enquiries through the guest's own email app and does not store them in a website database.
  • Information provided directly to the hotel may be used to manage reservations, guest services, safety and lawful records.
  • See the full Privacy Policy for more information.

Complaints & Management Decisions

  • Please raise concerns promptly so management has a fair opportunity to resolve them.
  • Policies may be updated as hotel operations develop.
  • All policies are subject to applicable local laws and any written terms confirmed with the guest.
Important: Prices, check-in/check-out times, cancellation charges and event-specific conditions should be confirmed directly with OOGLE HOTEL because they may vary by booking.
